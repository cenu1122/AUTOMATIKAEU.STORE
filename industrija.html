About Mechanical Calculators

This website provides simple and effective mechanical engineering calculators for students, engineers, and professionals. These tools are designed to help you quickly calculate important physical values such as force, power, and torque.

Hydraulic Force Calculator

The hydraulic force calculator uses the formula F = p × A, where pressure (p) multiplied by area (A) gives the resulting force (F). This is widely used in hydraulic systems such as presses, cylinders, and heavy machinery.

Power Calculator

Power is calculated using the formula P = F × v. It represents the rate at which work is done. This calculator is useful for determining motor power, mechanical output, and efficiency in various systems.

Torque Calculator

Torque is calculated using M = F × r. It represents rotational force and is used in engines, shafts, and mechanical components where rotation is involved.

Why Use These Tools?

These calculators are simple, fast, and accessible online. Whether you are a student learning engineering concepts or a professional working in industry, these tools can save time and improve accuracy.
